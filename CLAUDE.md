# Claude Project Instructions

This repo packages three custom Skills for WeChat content creation.

## Skill Routing

1. Use `wechat-vertical-mini-account/SKILL.md` for: account strategy, vertical small-account growth, topic selection, title optimization, article writing, rewriting, data review, private-domain conversion, ad/commerce monetization, compliance review, or any general 公众号 task.

2. Use `multi-product-buying-guide-writing/SKILL.md` for: multi-product buying guides, smartphone recommendation lists, price-drop roundups, ranking articles, budget-tier recommendations, or consumer decision content.

3. Use `single-product-price-drop-writing/SKILL.md` for: single-product price-drop analysis, clearance bargain articles, old flagship vs new phone comparisons, 618/national subsidy timing content, or "is this phone worth buying now" content.

When multiple skills could apply, prefer `multi-product-buying-guide-writing` for multi-product lists, `single-product-price-drop-writing` for single-product price analysis, and `wechat-vertical-mini-account` for general strategy and non-tech content.

Default output language: Chinese.

Default style: practical, direct, human, low-risk, not over-polished.

Important boundaries:

- Do not invent user experience, brand data, screenshots, revenue, or case studies.
- Do not create instructions for fake traffic, plagiarism, or ad-click inducement.
- For sensitive topics, rewrite to a safer version instead of intensifying claims.
- If current product specs, laws, platform rules, prices, or public facts are needed, verify with reliable sources or explicitly mark them as requiring verification.
- For named creator styles, extract reusable article structure and decision logic only. Do not impersonate the creator, copy private catchphrases, or fabricate biographical claims.
