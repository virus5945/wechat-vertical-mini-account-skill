# 单品降价捡漏导购写作

## 风格使用边界

这个 Skill 只提供单品降价文章结构和消费决策表达，不用于扮演或复刻某个真实创作者。

不要复制创作者的具体人设、固定私人口头禅、个人经历或高度可识别的句式指纹。如果用户要求“完全模仿”某个真实账号，改成更安全的版本：单品降价捡漏结构 + 价格锚点 + 适合谁/不适合谁 + 原创表达。

## Core Intent

Write like a price watcher and buying-decision assistant, not like a neutral spec sheet.

The reader wants to know:

- 现在买会不会亏
- 这台手机为什么突然值得看
- 价格、库存、配置里哪个点最关键
- 短板能不能接受
- 适合谁下手，谁应该等等

Default output should reduce decision anxiety. Give a concrete buying thesis, show the price context, translate specs into user benefits, and admit meaningful shortcomings.

## Verify Before Writing

When writing current buying advice, verify unstable facts first when possible:

- current price, national subsidy price, coupon price, stock status
- launch date, original price, version availability
- benchmark score, sales figure, ranking, good-review rate
- product specs, software support, certifications

If verification is not possible, use uncertainty markers such as:

- "目前公开信息显示"
- "电商参考价约"
- "以实际页面为准"
- "如果页面价格没变"
- "库存和活动价可能随平台变化"

Never invent exact prices, sales records, rankings, review rates, or subsidy amounts.

## Positioning

Use one clear article positioning per piece:

- **Price-drop opportunity**: old price versus current price, why the drop happened, whether it is worth buying.
- **Clearance bargain**: low stock, older model, rare version, why it may disappear soon.
- **Old flagship versus new phone**: prove the old model is still competitive at the new price.
- **Rare configuration**: 1TB, 24GB RAM, 10000mAh battery, IP69K, satellite communication, true full screen.
- **Brand or market event**: 618, national subsidy, memory price hike, new launch, official price cut.
- **Proof story**: sales record, good-review rate, shortage, user demand, historical low.

Avoid pure parameter dumping. Every paragraph should explain why the information changes the buying decision.

## Title Formula

Build titles from 4 parts:

`price/spec hook + event/status + product/category + emotional buying signal`

Common title ingredients:

- Price/spec hook: `16GB+512GB`, `1TB`, `10000mAh`, `IP69K`, `卫星通信`, `麒麟`, `骁龙`, `天玑`, `2K屏`
- Event/status: `降到`, `跌至`, `跳水`, `清仓`, `官宣`, `重回低价`, `发布不到两个月`, `首销到手价`
- Product/category: `华为手机`, `荣耀手机`, `小米手机`, `千元机`, `旧旗舰`, `折叠屏`
- Emotional signal: `值得捡漏`, `突然不香了`, `扛不住了`, `半价清仓`, `且买且珍惜`, `早买就是赚`

Good patterns:

- `16GB+1TB+2K屏，现已跌至2294元，618值得捡漏的一款小米手机`
- `从3799元跌至1996元，华为手机618半价清仓，512GB+卫星消息`
- `荣耀手机扛不住了，10080mAh+IP69K，512GB降到2379元`

Title rules:

- Use concrete numbers when the data is verified.
- Put the strongest price or spec before the product name.
- Use one main tension only: cheap, scarce, record-breaking, or new-versus-old.
- Do not promise "最低", "第一", "销量纪录", or "好评率99%" unless sourced.

## Opening Formula

Open with market context or reader pain, then connect it to the specific opportunity.

Recommended structure:

1. Start from a broader event or pain: memory price hike, 618, national subsidy, new launch, shortage, expensive new phones, user comments.
2. Explain why ordinary consumers care: afraid of buying expensive, wanting large storage, needing battery life, waiting for a historical low.
3. Reveal the target model and buying thesis.

Example template:

```text
最近手机价格变化很明显，尤其是大内存版本，因为元器件涨价，很多新机反而没有以前那么香。站在消费者角度，既然预算有限，就更应该盯住那些降价后的旧旗舰或者清仓机型。

这次要聊的这款手机就是这种情况。它当初定位不低，但现在因为新机上市和库存清仓，价格已经降到一个更容易接受的位置。如果你刚好在意[核心需求]，它确实值得单独拿出来说一说。
```

Use "阿维", "粉丝", "我之前提到过", "我一直在关注" only when matching an account-persona draft. Do not overuse them in every paragraph.

## Body Structure

Use a 6-7 paragraph single-model structure by default.

### 1. Market Setup

Explain the external reason this phone matters now:

- 618 or major promotion has started
- memory/storage price hike makes old high-storage versions valuable
- new phone launched and made old model cheaper
- official price cut or national subsidy changes value
- stock is low or only one version remains

### 2. Price Anchor

Give the original price, current reference price, and version:

```text
当初这款手机的[version]定价是[old price]，现在叠加活动后参考到手价约为[current price]。这个价格变化的关键不只是便宜，而是它把原本[price tier]的体验压到了[lower price tier]。
```

Always clarify whether the price is official, platform, coupon, national subsidy, or approximate.

### 3. Core Selling Point

Pick one main reason:

- battery and durability
- storage and long-term use
- screen and eye comfort
- performance and gaming
- camera and portraits
- satellite communication or weak-signal use
- foldable productivity or form-factor innovation

Lead with that one reason. Do not give equal weight to every spec.

### 4. Supporting Specs

Translate specs into user benefits:

- `10000mAh` -> fewer charges, outdoor/work delivery/gaming use
- `1TB` -> no storage anxiety, useful under storage price hike
- `IP69K` -> rain, splashes, harsh use, outdoor peace of mind
- `OIS/long telephoto` -> night shots, portraits, stage, travel
- `PWM/护眼` -> night reading and long screen time
- `flagship chip` -> longer smooth-use cycle, gaming, multitasking
- `wireless charging/satellite/NFC/IR` -> premium convenience

### 5. Honest Shortcomings

Include one clear shortcoming before the conclusion:

- old model, software cycle, stock limitation
- camera not flagship level
- battery small for current standards
- heavy body
- weak waterproofing
- performance no longer top
- system reputation or brand tradeoff

The shortcoming should sharpen the recommendation:

```text
它当然不是没有短板。拍照不是它的主场，机身也不算轻。所以它更适合看重续航和耐用的人，不适合把影像放在第一位的用户。
```

### 6. Buying Verdict

End with a usable decision:

- "预算在 X，重视 Y，可以考虑"
- "如果你更在意 Z，不如等下一波"
- "首销期/清仓期值得看，恢复原价后吸引力下降"
- "库存少，适合刚需用户，不适合犹豫型用户"

Optional comment prompt:

```text
这样一款[price/spec]手机，你们觉得现在值不值得买？
```

## Language Style

Use conversational Chinese with high information density.

Useful phrases:

- "站在消费者的角度"
- "这也是它现在值得看的原因"
- "不是说它没有短板"
- "关键要结合价格来看"
- "如果你的刚需是..."
- "这个价格确实更容易接受"
- "后续会不会继续降不好说"
- "库存和活动价还是要看实际页面"

Use scarcity and timing sparingly:

- "清仓"
- "捡漏"
- "且买且珍惜"
- "重回低价"
- "跌破"
- "跳水"
- "首销期"

Avoid:

- too many exclamation marks
- unsupported lowest-price, only-choice, or ranking claims
- pretending hands-on testing without real testing
- hiding drawbacks at the very end after overpraising
- repeating the same opening in multiple articles

## Trust Moves

Build credibility by doing these:

- Mention how the price was checked: official price, platform price, coupon, national subsidy.
- Explain why the phone became cheaper: new model, clearance, stock, brand pressure, promotion.
- Compare with a nearby alternative when relevant.
- Say "不建议盲目入手" for expensive or niche models.
- Say "适合谁、不适合谁" rather than "闭眼入".
- Treat exact prices as time-sensitive.

## Quick Draft Checklist

Before finalizing an article, ensure it has:

- A title with verified number plus clear buying signal.
- A first paragraph tied to market context or reader anxiety.
- A price anchor with original/current price and version.
- One main buying thesis.
- Specs translated into daily-use value.
- One honest shortcoming.
- A concrete verdict for the target reader.

## Output Shapes

For a full article, use 6-8 compact paragraphs with no Markdown table unless the user requests comparison.

For an outline, return:

- title options
- core buying thesis
- paragraph plan
- shortcoming angle
- verdict sentence

For critique, lead with:

- title strength
- opening hook
- price/value proof
- missing trust details
- recommendation clarity
